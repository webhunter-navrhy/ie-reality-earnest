Earnest are delighted to present Apt. 28 Timber Mills to the sales market. Timber Mills is a well-maintained and popular residential development, conveniently located adjacent to Artane Shopping Centre. This well-positioned dual-aspect two-bedroom apartment is situated on the 1st floor and offers bright and well-proportioned accommodation, with a private south-west facing rear terrace leading down to its own garden, a front balcony and two designated car parking spaces. The apartment also benefits from new laminate flooring throughout the living area, hall and bedrooms.

The accommodation briefly comprises a welcoming entrance hallway leading into a bright, south-west facing living room. Double doors open directly from the living room onto the rear terrace and garden. The kitchen is located just off the living room and features ample wall and floor units, a tiled splashback and tiled flooring.

The hallway continues to the two bedrooms, both of which have built-in wardrobes. The main bedroom is a generous double and enjoys a large private balcony. There is excellent storage throughout the apartment, including two purpose-built storage rooms, while the bathroom and separate hot press complete the accommodation.

Externally, the property benefits from a front balcony and a south-west facing rear terrace off the living area, enjoying a sunny aspect ideal for outdoor dining or relaxing. Steps lead down to the private rear garden, laid in artificial lawn and bordered by mature shrubs.

The location is highly convenient, with Artane Shopping Centre right on the doorstep, providing a wide range of shops, services and amenities. The area is also well served by public transport and offers easy access to Dublin City Centre and surrounding areas.
